# Neutral

## Overview
These entities, so called **Demons**, **Npcs** or **Neutrals**, attack only if provoked by a Player, Pet or Tower. They spawn up to 3 around their Neutral Camp, a red looking rock. 

<ImageWithDescription image="/asset/gameplay/game/entities/npcs.png" description="Neutrals around their Neutral Camp" />

## Behaviour
- Neutrals spawn in a 200x200px area (square) around the Neutral Camp.
- Neutrals respawn after 32.5 seconds (650 ticks)
- They have an attacking radius of 1000x1000px.
- Once the target leaves, neutrals return within the 300x300px area of their original spawn, NOT Neutral camp.
- They cannot be pushed out with ease out of their spawn range, they will try to return back.
- They always face towards their original spawn point, NOT Neutral Camp.

You can also view this information in the following illustration.

<ImageWithDescription image="/asset/gameplay/game/entities/npc_behaviour.png" description="Neutrals behaviour" />

## Use
They're generally useless, used only to level up your pet C.A.R.L. with EXP points. They're also used to raid bases, only if the attacked tower is within the 1000x1000 attack radius.
